TIKR looks like a junior analyst's workstation: income statement, segments, estimates, then the earnings call. The free plan is a US teaser with shorter history. **Plus** is the first plan that looks like global research rather than a demo.

We cap this directory at Plus. Pro and Ultimate add longer history, more transcript archives, and extra estimate depth. Those may be worth it; they are not automatically still "budget." Always check the live Plus price, because third-party reviews in 2026 still disagree by a few dollars.

What Plus is not: a broker, a real-time day-trading tape, or a written buy/sell call. You bring the judgment. TIKR brings the statements.

If your universe is only US large caps and you already have Finviz plus a 10-K habit, Plus may be redundant. If you read European and Asian names, this is usually the listing that earns the tab.
