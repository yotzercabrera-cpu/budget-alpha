Seeking Alpha Premium is a **research platform**, not a single-author newsletter: unlimited contributor analysis plus Quant Ratings, screeners, and AI research helpers. High-volume ideas with a proprietary ratings layer — between a newsletter and a data tool.

We list **$299/year** (~$24.92/mo) as the directory price because that is the **renewal**. The common new-subscriber tease is **$4.95 for the first month**, then auto-renew at $299/yr. There is **no true month-to-month** after the intro. Taxes/VAT are extra. Occasional first-year affiliate deals (~$269) still renew at then-current list. **PRO** (~$2,400/yr) and **Alpha Picks** (~$499/yr) are separate — PRO is over the directory cap and is not listed here.

What you get (public claims): unlimited Premium author analysis, Quant Ratings and Factor Grades, stock/ETF screeners, AI helpers (summaries, earnings-call insights, Ask Seeking Alpha), dividend scores, peer comps, and portfolio alerts. Free Basic exists; the useful research is mostly behind Premium.

Best if you want breadth (many tickers, many authors) plus a ratings/screener layer under ~$25/mo effective. Skip if you want one analyst’s voice, a pure DCF workspace, or month-to-month billing after a trial.

We do **not** verify Quant outperformance claims or author track records. Article quality is uneven because it is crowd-sourced. Quant Ratings are model estimates — not advice and not independently audited here. Cancel before the intro month ends if you do not want the annual charge; confirm refund Terms at signup.
