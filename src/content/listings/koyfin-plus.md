Koyfin is the retail terminal aesthetic: multi-panel graphs, macro dashboards, earnings, and a screener that wants custom columns. The free plan is a real start. **Plus** is the self-directed paid rung before Premium's deeper analytics.

Budget Alpha lists Plus because **$39/month** is under $50. We leave `priceAnnual` blank on purpose. Inventing a yearly total from `12 × 39` would ignore whatever discount Koyfin shows on annual checkout. If the annual invoice is at or under about $500, it still fits; if a "Plus" badge is actually Premium, it does not.

Do not buy Plus as a personality. Buy it if you will keep a dashboard open. Overlap with TradingView (charts) and TIKR (statements) is real. Koyfin's edge is **assembled views** — yield curves next to earnings next to a portfolio — at a non-Advisor price.

Refunds: Koyfin publicly advertises a 30-day satisfaction window. That is a cooling-off policy, not a performance guarantee. We do not claim you will earn the subscription back.
