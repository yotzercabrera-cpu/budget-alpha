Simply Wall St turns fundamentals into a **Snowflake** and a short narrative. That is the product. It is friendlier than a raw screener and less opinionated than a human newsletter.

Premium is listed because **$131.40/year** is comfortably inside the cap and the monthly rate is $15.99. Unlimited costs more and removes some metering; we still mention it because $258/year would qualify, but the directory entry is Premium so the SKU stays honest.

The risk is not the price. The risk is **believing the graphic**. Health, value, and "future growth" axes are computed. They can be a useful checklist. They are not a forecast we stand behind, and Simply Wall St should not be read as if it were an analyst who will be wrong in an interesting way.

If you already live in TIKR statements or Stock Rover columns, Premium can feel like a children's book. If you freeze in front of a 10-K, it is often the right first tab — then you graduate.
