Stock Rover is built for people who think in columns: return on capital, dividend growth, valuation ratios, then a portfolio that should match the brokerage. Premium is the independent-investor tier — enough metrics and history for serious screening without crossing into Premium Plus.

Budget Alpha only lists **Premium**. Premium Plus and Ultimate are useful products; they are not this directory's price band. If a sales page pushes you up a tier, treat that as a different purchase.

Coverage is **North America**, not a global filings terminal. Charting is serviceable, not TradingView. There is no human-written stock pitch hiding in the scores. The product is data density and portfolio plumbing.

Price discipline: $34/month or $348/year. The annual plan is the ordinary cheaper cadence, not an introductory teaser we could find. Still, read the renewal line. Multi-year prepay changes the cash timing, not the fact that you are buying a recurring research seat.
