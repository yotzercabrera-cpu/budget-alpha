Finimize sits on both sides of this directory: it is a **newsletter** most people should keep free, and a **Pro** product that some readers will pay for. We file it as `both`.

The free daily brief is the habit. Pro is extra commentary and research-shaped writing. We are not going to pretend Pro is a hedge-fund feed. It is closer to paying for a well-produced explainer magazine than for Stock Rover columns.

US prices we last logged: **$29.99/month or $200/year**. That annual number is the one that usually makes sense; twelve monthly charges would be higher. Other countries see different stickers. Always read the local checkout.

The trial is the gotcha. Annual Pro has been sold with **seven free days that become a charge**. If you wanted to "just look," set a reminder. We do not list Finimize because it beats the market. We list it because the published Pro rates clear the cap and the free tier is a legitimate product, not a crippled decoy.
