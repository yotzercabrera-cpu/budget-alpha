Behind the Balance Sheet is Stephen Clapham's Substack: **how to look at companies**, with the tone of someone who used to do it for a living and now also teaches.

The price history is unusually well documented by the writer himself: start too cheap, raise, then set **$35/month alongside $350/year** so the annual plan is not a weird bargain versus a month of curiosity around one issue. We use that pair. Checkout may have moved.

This listing is the **newsletter**, not the entire Clapham universe. Books, courses, and events can be worth their own budget line. They are how a "budget" research habit accidentally becomes a four-figure year.

We will not recap stock ideas from the archive as if they were our picks. If a premium post names a company, that is his argument on his date, not a Budget Alpha recommendation and not evidence of subscriber returns.

Substack auto-renews. If you came for one issue, cancel before the next bill. That is allowed. It is also why publishers tighten monthly/annual gaps.
