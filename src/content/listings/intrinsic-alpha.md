Intrinsic Alpha is a value-investing research **tool**, not a newsletter. Public positioning: estimate what a business is worth, edit DCF assumptions, document the thesis, and wait for a margin-of-safety buy zone — rather than chasing tips.

We list **Premium** as the entry paid plan at **$10/month or $110/year** (~8% off vs paying monthly). **Unlimited** sits at $15/mo or $165/yr for people who blow through weekly caps; it stays optional, not the directory list price. Free exists at $0. A 7-day Premium trial is claimed with no credit card; 30-day money-back applies to annual only. Annual amounts live in the pricing FAQ more clearly than on the primary cards — double-check the checkout toggle.

What you get is an intrinsic-value / stock-report workflow, editable DCFs (basic on Premium; sensitivity analysis on Unlimited), watchlists and alerts, a screener, AI prompts with tier caps, and thesis notes so assumptions stay with the company. The site markets broad global coverage; what you actually see still depends on filings, estimates, and data availability.

Best if you already think in intrinsic value and margin of safety and want one workspace instead of scattered spreadsheets. Skip if you want a curated tip newsletter, or if you need uncapped weekly volume on the cheapest paid tier — that is Unlimited's job.

We are **not** verifying data accuracy, model quality, or any implied edge. Site frames valuations as **estimates, not predictions**. No audited performance track record is reviewed here. Confirm Free/Premium weekly limits in the live account UI before you optimize around them.
