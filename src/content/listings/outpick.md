Outpick sells intentional, value-style research for people who have outgrown index-only investing and do not want a trade-alert service. They research businesses worth owning for years, publish on a schedule, and run an example book in the open — including exits and losses.

We list one plan because that is all they advertise: **$250/year**, flat, no percent of assets, no monthly option. That is about **$20.83/month** if you annualize — clearly inside the directory cap. There is no public tier ladder and no "then renews at…" bait on the marketing page.

What you pay for is a biweekly high-conviction note from a ~3,600 US-listed universe, written as a full thesis, plus a live example portfolio with notes when positions open or close. A free Monday model note gives sector and cycle color; membership picks stay behind the paywall. They state clearly what they do **not** do: trading strategies, market timing, entry/exit prices, or personal advice.

Best if you want research you can read and decide on yourself, and you are okay locking in a year. Skip if you need monthly billing, non-US coverage as the core product, or mechanical buy/sell alerts.

We are **not** verifying any return figures on their marketing pages. Coverage is pitched as US-listed — do not assume global. Read samples before you pay.
