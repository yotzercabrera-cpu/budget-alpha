ValueInvesting.io is a **valuation and research tool**, not a newsletter. Public pitch: pre-built intrinsic-value and DCF models for a large equity universe, plus screener, watchlists, charting, and AI-assisted filings search — for DIY value investors who do not want to rebuild every model from scratch.

We list the **Personal regional** plan billed annually as the directory price: **$14.99/mo ≈ $180/year**. **Global Personal** is **$42.99/mo billed annually (~$516/yr)** — still under **$50/month**, slightly over a strict ~$500/year reading of the soft annual line. We keep Global in the notes, not as the headline list price. Stickers are Personal-use and exclude ~**10% tax**. A **7-day trial** is offered on valuation subscriptions. Month-to-month is **not** on the current homepage; older ~$19.99/mo cites are unverified — believe checkout.

What you get (public claims): editable DCF / intrinsic-value estimates, a screener, watchlists, charting, Excel/Sheets export, and AI filings search. Coverage is marketed at **45,000+ stocks / ~60 exchanges**, but **region gating** means the cheap regional plan is not the full global universe. Free exists with capped views.

Best if you already think in intrinsic value and want speed without a terminal. Skip if you want curated picks, need a confirmed cheap month-to-month exit, or need Global coverage on a hard ≤~$500/yr budget.

We are **not** verifying model accuracy, data freshness, or any implied edge. Valuations are **estimates**, not a performance track record. Thematic overlap with Intrinsic Alpha is real — compare UI, coverage, and export on a trial. Auto-renew, tax, and **no-refund** Terms matter; cancel before renewal if you are done.
