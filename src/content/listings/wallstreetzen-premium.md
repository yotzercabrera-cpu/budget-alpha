WallStreetZen Premium is a **fundamentals research desk**: Zen Ratings, an advanced stock screener, and a ranked database of Wall Street analyst forecasts. Free Basic stays available, but it caps due diligence checks, analyst performance pages, and “Why Price Moved” explanations, and it still shows ads. Premium lifts those caps, adds screener export, and drops the ads. It is a research tooling layer, not a single-author newsletter and not a broker.

We list it on the **annual bill**. As of 2026-09-23, WallStreetZen’s own materials put Premium at **$59 per month** or **$234 per year** ($19.50/mo billed annually). Monthly $59 sits over Budget Alpha’s $50 comfort line. Annual $234 clears the ~$500/yr cap, which is why it is in the directory. The plans page leads with the $19.50 annual-equivalent rate and a low-dollar trial (often framed as try for $1). That intro is not the price — it converts to the then-current monthly or annual rate. Confirm checkout and any money-back window before you pay. Zen Investor and other WallStreetZen products are separate SKUs.

We do **not** independently verify WallStreetZen’s marketed track record, compounding anecdotes, or claims that its ratings or top analysts outperform. Treat those figures as vendor marketing. Ratings and analyst rankings are a structured way to filter names, not a result we will reprint.

Seeking Alpha Premium is the closer peer if you want a high-volume article stream plus quant tools. Finviz Elite is the one for US heat maps and a fast tape. Morningstar Investor is the one for fund research and the firm’s fair-value and rating language. WallStreetZen sits with the fundamentals screeners: ratings, filters, and analyst forecasts, at about $19.50/mo effective when you pay the year up front.
