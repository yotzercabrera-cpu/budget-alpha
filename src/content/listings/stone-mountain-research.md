Stone Mountain Research is an independent Substack focused on deep fundamental work into underfollowed and "boring compounder" equities. Free readers get selected pieces. Paid unlocks the full report set, the research pipeline, a live watchlist, and the author's buy and sell signals.

We list it at **$40/month or $200/year** — the pair on the Substack upgrade UI at review. Annual is the clear value (~$16.67/mo) versus **$480/year** if you stay monthly. Older posts mentioned **$30/mo** and a possible later-2026 hike; neither showed on the live upgrade page. Believe checkout.

Paid readers get complete deep dives (model, financials, management, competition, risk), often with audio walkthroughs, plus names under study before they publish, a watchlist with targets, and live signals when the author acts. Marketing also claims early access to **AlphaIQ** (screener, financials, DCF) — previously marked paused; treat that as provisional until you confirm it is live.

Best if you want someone else to grind filings on smaller names and still prefer to decide yourself. Skip if you need a multi-analyst desk, global coverage as the core product, or a mature always-on research terminal bundled in.

Single-author concentration is real. We do **not** verify performance of any signals or published ideas. Read free samples before you pay.
