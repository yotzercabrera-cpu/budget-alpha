MarketBeat is a retail aggregator of analyst ratings, upgrades and downgrades, insider and institutional activity, and a pair of daily newsletters sitting on that feed. All Access is the paid seat. It is not a broker, not a DCF terminal, and not a Motley Fool-style pick service with a track record we endorse.

The free **Daily Ratings** plan still exists. All Access is the denser layer — twice-daily priority newsletters, unlimited watchlists and alerts, extra screener filters, Idea Engine, transcripts, and export. You are buying coverage cadence and monitoring, not a new research philosophy.

What it is **not**: a workstation for 10-year financial models, a heat map of the US tape, or a single-author newsletter with a verified edge. Idea Engine marketing sometimes shows cherry-picked return slides. Treat those as product demos, not evidence the subscription works.

Price discipline: official list is **$29/month or $249/year**, verified on MarketBeat's All Access page and FAQ (2026-09-21). Annual billing saves $99 versus twelve monthly invoices. Promotional checkout links sometimes advertise ~$19.99/mo or $199/yr for a first term — those are not the standing retail rate we list, and they are not the renewal you should assume. A 30-day free trial (card required) auto-converts unless you cancel; paying immediately gets a 30-day money-back window.

Compare it to **Finviz** if you want heat maps and a fast US screener, **Stock Rover** if you live in fundamentals, or **Seeking Alpha** if you want long-form crowd research. MarketBeat wins on ratings alerts and a cheaper newsletter cadence. It loses on charting, deep financials, and original analysis.
