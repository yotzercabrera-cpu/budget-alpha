Net Interest is Marc Rubinstein writing about **financial firms** as businesses: balance sheets, incentives, history, then the current setup. It is closer to industry research than to a markets column.

The individual subscription has been discussed by the author at **$25/month or $250/year**. That clears Budget Alpha's cap with room. The institutional product does not belong here; it is a research-budget line item.

Free posts exist so you can test the voice. Paid is for the rest of the archive and the fuller cadence. There is no accompanying data terminal.

Renewal is ordinary Substack behavior: you are charged again unless you cancel. If you signed up during a cheaper moment, you may be grandfathered. New subscribers should not assume they get a veteran's rate.

We list Net Interest because the public individual price qualifies and the beat is specific. Specific is the point. A general "markets wrap" this is not.
