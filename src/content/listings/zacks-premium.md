Zacks Premium is Zacks Investment Research’s paid **research and screening membership** — Zacks Rank, earnings-estimate revisions, Style Scores, premium screens, and equity research reports. Tools-and-research product, not a single-author newsletter.

We list **$249/year** (~$20.75/mo effective) because that is the **live Premium sticker** (verified 2026-09-20). There is **no month-to-month Premium** price on the page. A **30-day free trial** is advertised; after that it converts to the annual plan. An advertised refund window runs up to **90 days after trial** — confirm Terms at signup. **Investor Collection** and **Ultimate** are separate; Ultimate is over the directory cap. The site’s “beat the market / performance guarantee” language is **marketing**, not a verified edge we endorse.

What you get (public claims): fuller Zacks Rank / estimate-revision tooling, Style Scores, premium screens, research reports and Focus List-style content, plus portfolio/alert features marketed with Premium. A free tier still exists with limited Rank lookups.

Best if you already care about analyst estimate revisions and want Rank + screens under ~$21/mo effective. Skip if you need month-to-month billing, a DCF workshop, or Ultimate’s higher-tier portfolios (over cap).

We do **not** verify historical Rank performance tables or guarantee copy. At checkout, stick to **Premium** — higher tiers are easy to confuse with this directory entry.
