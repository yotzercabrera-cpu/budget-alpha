The Diff is Byrne Hobart's newsletter about **variance**: companies, markets, and technologies where the distribution of outcomes is wide. Free subscribers get a sample of the weekday cadence. Paid subscribers get the rest of the week.

We list it as a newsletter, not a tool. There is no screener hiding behind the paywall. The purchase is time with a particular writer.

Pricing honesty: public archive posts announced **$20/month or $220/year** for new subscribers after June 2020, up from $15 / $150. That is the last clean public pair we will print. If checkout now shows something else, believe checkout. Founding members may still be on older rates; that is a grandfather, not an "intro year then $499."

We will not claim The Diff "generates alpha." It generates sentences. If those sentences change how you see a business, that is on you and your process.
