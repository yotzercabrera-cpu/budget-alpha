TradingView is where a lot of the world's charts live, including crypto, futures, and stocks. The free Basic plan is genuinely usable. Essential is for when Basic's **one-chart, thin-alert, ad-supported** ceiling starts to interrupt the work.

We list Essential because it is clearly inside the cap at about **$15/month or $155/year**. Higher tiers exist because people want more panes, more alerts, and more historical bars — not because Essential is a fake "lite" SKU. If you need Volume Profile, many chart windows, or second-based data, you are shopping a different (often still sub-$50) Plus plan, or you are about to leave this directory's comfort zone on Premium.

Two costs hide next to the subscription. **Exchange real-time data** is frequently sold on top of the software seat. And **annual specials** on the pricing page are not the same thing as the public list we quote. If checkout is cheaper than $14.95, assume you need to know the renewal number.

This is charting infrastructure. It is not a stock-picking newsletter and it is not a 10-year fundamental modeler.
