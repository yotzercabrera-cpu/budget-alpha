Morningstar Investor is the consumer research seat: **stocks, funds, ETFs, and a look through your portfolio**, with the firm's ratings and fair-value language on covered securities.

It belongs here because **$249/year** is under ~$500 and **$34.95/month** is under $50. The annual plan is the one that matches how people actually use Morningstar — occasionally, then in bursts at tax or rebalance time. Paying monthly for that pattern is how you accidentally spend $419.

When you see **$199** (or another first-year number), treat it as an introduction unless the terms say the price is locked. Budget Alpha's cap is judged on the renewal we expect you to face, not on a landing-page hero number.

Stars, medals, and fair values are **opinions built on models**. They are not a track record we will reprint, and they are not a substitute for reading the prospectus. If you wanted only a visual Snowflake, Simply Wall St is cheaper. If you wanted raw statements, TIKR is the other direction.

Do not buy Investor because you meant to buy **Morningstar Direct**. Read the product name on the invoice.
