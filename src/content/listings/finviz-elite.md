Finviz is the map most US retail traders already know: a dense heat map, a huge free screener, and quote pages that load faster than a terminal. Elite is the paid layer on top of that map.

What you are buying is **timeliness and limits**, not a new research philosophy. The free site is delayed and caps how far you can push screens, charts, and exports. Elite unlocks real-time quotes, more screener depth, alerts, and export. It does not write a thesis for you and it does not cover global filings the way TIKR does.

We list Elite because the **published annual rate ($299.50)** sits well under the Budget Alpha cap, and the monthly rate ($39.50) also clears $50. We are not claiming Elite pays for itself in better trades. It is a cost of attention: you pay to see the US tape and the screen without waiting on the delay.

Compare it to Stock Rover if you live in fundamentals, or TradingView if you live on charts. Finviz wins on **glanceable market structure**. It loses on portfolio accounting, transcripts, and international depth.
